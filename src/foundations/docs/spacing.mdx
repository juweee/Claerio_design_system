# Use spacing, radius and layout

Spacing, radius, shadows and layout widths are tokens in `design-tokens.css`. The "Foundations/Spacing, radius and shadows" stories show them.

## Space things on the scale

| Token | Value | Tailwind |
|---|---|---|
| `--space-1` | 4px | `p-1`, `gap-1` |
| `--space-2` | 8px | `p-2`, `gap-2` |
| `--space-3` | 12px | `p-3`, `gap-3` |
| `--space-4` | 16px | `p-4`, `gap-4` |
| `--space-6` | 24px | `p-6`, `gap-6` |
| `--space-8` | 32px | `p-8`, `gap-8` |
| `--space-12` | 48px | `p-12`, `gap-12` |

Tailwind's spacing steps are 4px, so `p-6` is 24px. Stay on the values above.

## Lay out a page

- The app shell is at most `--layout-max-width` (1280px) wide, centered: `mx-auto max-w-(--layout-max-width)`.
- The shell is a navigation rail, then a column of PageHeader, `<main>` and `<footer>`. The header and footer sit outside `<main>`.
- The page body has 24px padding and 24px between sections: `p-6 gap-6`.
- Cards have 16px padding (Card applies it). Put a FilterRow under the card title, 12px above what it filters.
- The "Page header" component's "App shell" stories show the whole frame.

## Round corners

| Token | Value | Utility | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--radius-sm` | 4px | `rounded-sm` | Tooltips and small parts, such as focus outlines on text |
| `--radius-md` | 8px | `rounded-md` | Buttons, inputs, filters and menus |
| `--radius-lg` | 12px | `rounded-lg` | Cards, modals and toasts |
| `--radius-full` | 9999px | `rounded-full` | Avatars, chips |

Use only these 4 values. For a rounded shape inside another one, the inner radius is the outer radius minus the gap between them.

## Use shadows only on overlays

- `--shadow-overlay` (`shadow-overlay`): toasts and menus.
- `--shadow-modal` (`shadow-modal`): modals only. It's `none` in dark mode.
- Cards have no shadow, in either mode.

## Show focus the same way everywhere

Focus-visible is a 2px `--ring` outline with a 2px offset: `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-ring`.
